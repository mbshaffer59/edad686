The most important course you didn’t even know you needed — and will walk away loving.
“

A President of the United States once observed — correctly, if not prophetically — that “the ability to be cool, confident, and decisive in crisis is not an inherited characteristic but is the direct result of how well the individual has prepared himself for the battle.”

— A President of the United States, who knew this to be true, believed himself exempt from it, and spent the remainder of his public life proving the point.

Most landmark education cases didn’t have to go that far. A principal told the Tinker children they couldn’t wear black armbands to protest a war. The Supreme Court disagreed — unanimously, permanently, and on the first page of every school law textbook ever printed. We don’t remember the principal’s name. We remember the Tinkers.

Somewhere in your career is a decision that will define you the same way. This course is the difference between making it wisely — and making it into a case study.

Welcome to EDAD 686. Prepare accordingly.

— M.B. Shaffer
